About Us

Proprietor David Bidwell

I have worked for 40 years in the tree industry, including looking after the collection of nearly 4000 trees at Sydney's Royal Botanic Gardens and Domain, where I worked for 24 years as Senior Arborist. I have worked and collaborated with colleagues across Australia and overseas. Since establishing Rainbow Tree Consultancy in 2017, I have worked as an arboricultural consultant on multiple projects including in urban parks, schools, national parks, major civil projects, and suburban backyards. I regularly lecture and train in the industry.

Qualifications

  • Diploma of Arboriculture (AQF Level 5)
  • QTRA (Quantified Tree Risk Assessment) assessor.
  • TRAQ (Tree Risk Assessment Qualification) assessor.
  • Certificate IV, OH&S (WHS)
  • First Aider since 1993.
  • Workcover “White” card
