Services

Consultations

We will undertake consultations, and are able to provide reports on all matters relating to trees.

Tree Assessments

We are qualified and experienced in carrying out Inspections, assessments, surveys on individual trees or groups of trees. We can report on tree health and condition, tree risk, estimated life expectancy,

Tree Risk

One of our areas of expertise is in the area of hazard identification, risk assessment, and giving advice on risk management. I am qualified in Quantified Tree Risk Assessment (QTRA), and Tree Risk assessment Qualification (TRAQ). These are the 2 most widely used tree risk assessment methodologies used in the tree industry worldwide.

Tree Care

We have extensive experience in managing trees in the urban environment. We are able to advise on tree selection, planting, and the aftercare of establishing trees. We specialise on the care, maintenance and management of semi-mature, mature and veteran trees.

We can identify and advise on the treatment of tree pests, diseases and other pathogens.

Advice on Best Practice

Rainbow Tree Consultancy can advise on tree pruning in relation to specifications set out in Australian Standards, AS4373-2007 – Pruning of Amenity Trees, and make recommendations in relation to trees and development, and AS4970-2025 – Protection of trees on Development Sites.
